MAINTENANCE AND SUPPORT AGREEMENT
============================================================

Parties: Evergreen Cyber Solutions Inc. and Harborlight Media Corp.
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Contract Value: $724,000
Governing Jurisdiction: the State of Delaware

------------------------------------------------------------

SECTION 1 DEFINITIONS

For the purposes of this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below.
'Affiliate' means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under
common control with a party, where 'control' means the ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of
the voting securities of such entity. 'Confidential Information' means all non-public information
disclosed by one party to the other, whether orally, in writing, or by inspection of tangible
objects, that is designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be
confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure. 'Deliverables'
means all reports, documents, software, and other materials prepared by the service provider for the
client under this Agreement. 'Effective Date' means the date first written above. 'Term' means the
period commencing on the Effective Date and continuing until expiration or termination in accordance
with the termination provisions of this Agreement. 'Personal Data' means any information relating to
an identified or identifiable natural person. 'Services' means the services described in one or more
statements of work executed by the parties from time to time. All other capitalized terms shall have
the meanings ascribed to them elsewhere in this Agreement or in the applicable statement of work.


SECTION 2 PAYMENT

Evergreen Cyber Solutions Inc. shall pay Harborlight Media Corp. the fees set forth in the
applicable statement of work in accordance with the following terms. Unless otherwise specified in
the applicable statement of work, all undisputed invoices are due and payable within 60 days from
the date of receipt. Payments shall be made in U.S. dollars by wire transfer to an account
designated in writing by Harborlight Media Corp.. Any amounts not paid when due shall accrue
interest at the lesser of (i) 1 percent (1%) per month, or (ii) the maximum rate permitted by
applicable law, calculated from the due date until paid in full. Evergreen Cyber Solutions Inc.
shall be responsible for all sales, use, and similar taxes arising from this Agreement, excluding
taxes based on Harborlight Media Corp.'s net income. Harborlight Media Corp. reserves the right to
suspend performance if any undisputed invoice remains unpaid for more than thirty (30) days after
the due date. The total contract value shall not exceed $724,000 without a written amendment signed
by both parties.


SECTION 3 CONFIDENTIALITY

Each party agrees to hold in strict confidence all Confidential Information of the other party and
to use such information solely for the purpose of performing its obligations under this Agreement.
The receiving party shall protect the disclosing party's Confidential Information using at least the
same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information, but in no event less than a
reasonable degree of care. The receiving party may disclose Confidential Information only to those
of its employees, contractors, and advisors who have a bona fide need to know such information and
who are bound by written obligations of confidentiality at least as protective as those contained
herein. The obligations set forth in this Section shall survive termination or expiration of this
Agreement for such period as the disclosing party may reasonably require to protect its Confidential
Information. Confidential Information does not include information that: (i) is or becomes publicly
available through no fault of the receiving party; (ii) was rightfully in the receiving party's
possession prior to disclosure; (iii) is rightfully obtained from a third party without restriction;
or (iv) is independently developed by the receiving party without use of or reference to the
disclosing party's Confidential Information.


SECTION 4 DATA PROTECTION

Harborlight Media Corp. shall process Personal Data only on documented instructions from Evergreen
Cyber Solutions Inc. and shall not transfer Personal Data to any third country or international
organization without prior written authorization from Evergreen Cyber Solutions Inc.. Harborlight
Media Corp. shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of
security appropriate to the risk, including as appropriate: encryption of Personal Data; the ability
to ensure ongoing confidentiality, integrity, availability, and resilience of processing systems;
and a process for regularly testing and evaluating the effectiveness of security measures.
Harborlight Media Corp. shall assist Evergreen Cyber Solutions Inc. in ensuring compliance with
obligations relating to security of processing, notification of personal data breaches, and data
protection impact assessments. Harborlight Media Corp. shall notify Evergreen Cyber Solutions Inc.
without undue delay, and in any event within 72 hours, after becoming aware of a Personal Data
breach affecting Personal Data processed under this Agreement. Upon termination of this Agreement,
Harborlight Media Corp. shall delete or return all Personal Data at Evergreen Cyber Solutions Inc.'s
choice, unless applicable law requires storage of such Personal Data.


SECTION 5 INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

All intellectual property rights in any materials, software, documentation, or other deliverables
created by Harborlight Media Corp. specifically for Evergreen Cyber Solutions Inc. under this
Agreement shall be owned exclusively by Evergreen Cyber Solutions Inc., effective upon full payment
of all fees associated with such deliverables. Harborlight Media Corp. hereby assigns to Evergreen
Cyber Solutions Inc. all right, title, and interest in and to such deliverables, and agrees to
execute such further documents as may be reasonably necessary to perfect such assignment.
Notwithstanding the foregoing, Harborlight Media Corp. retains all rights in its pre-existing
materials, tools, methodologies, and know-how, and grants Evergreen Cyber Solutions Inc. a non-
exclusive, worldwide, perpetual, royalty-free license to use such pre-existing materials solely as
embedded in the deliverables. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as granting either party
any rights in the other party's trademarks, service marks, or trade names, except as expressly set
forth in a separate written agreement executed by both parties.


SECTION 6 INDEMNIFICATION

Harborlight Media Corp. shall indemnify and hold harmless Evergreen Cyber Solutions Inc. and its
officers, directors, employees, and agents from and against any and all claims, damages, losses,
liabilities, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or relating
to: (i) any breach of this Agreement by Harborlight Media Corp.; (ii) any negligent or wrongful act
or omission of Harborlight Media Corp.; or (iii) any claim that the deliverables infringe the
intellectual property rights of a third party. Evergreen Cyber Solutions Inc. shall promptly notify
Harborlight Media Corp. of any such claim and shall provide reasonable cooperation in the defense
thereof. The obligations under this Section shall be subject to the limitation of liability set
forth in this Agreement. Harborlight Media Corp. shall have the right to control the defense of any
indemnified claim, provided that Harborlight Media Corp. shall not settle any claim in a manner that
imposes any obligation on Evergreen Cyber Solutions Inc. without Evergreen Cyber Solutions Inc.'s
prior written consent.


SECTION 7 LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

Each party's total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall be limited to the total fees paid
or payable by Evergreen Cyber Solutions Inc. to Harborlight Media Corp. under this Agreement during
the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. The parties acknowledge that
damages may include indirect, incidental, special, and consequential damages, and no such damages
shall be excluded under this Agreement. The limitations set forth in this Section shall not apply
to: (i) a party's indemnification obligations; (ii) breach of confidentiality obligations; (iii)
gross negligence or willful misconduct; or (iv) any liability that cannot be limited under
applicable law. The parties acknowledge that the limitations and exclusions set forth in this
Section reflect a mutual agreement and form an essential basis of the bargain between the parties.


SECTION 8 TERMINATION

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue until terminated in accordance with
this Section. Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by delivering written notice
to the other party, and such termination shall be effective immediately upon delivery of the notice,
without any cure period or advance notice requirement. Either party may terminate this Agreement
immediately upon written notice if the other party: (i) materially breaches this Agreement and fails
to cure such breach within thirty (30) days after receipt of written notice of such breach; or (ii)
becomes insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes subject to any
proceeding under bankruptcy or insolvency law. Upon termination of this Agreement, Evergreen Cyber
Solutions Inc. shall pay Harborlight Media Corp. for all services performed and expenses reasonably
incurred through the effective date of termination. The provisions of this Agreement relating to
confidentiality, intellectual property, indemnification, limitation of liability, and governing law
shall survive any termination or expiration of this Agreement.


SECTION 9 GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of
Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The parties irrevocably submit to the
exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the State of Delaware for any
dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and each party waives any objection to venue
in such courts.


SECTION 10 DISPUTE RESOLUTION

Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall first be subject to good-faith
negotiation between senior representatives of the parties for a period of thirty (30) days following
written notice of the dispute. If the dispute is not resolved through such negotiation, it shall be
finally resolved by binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association under
its Commercial Arbitration Rules, including its Emergency Measures procedures. The arbitration shall
take place in a mutually agreed location, and the award rendered by the arbitrator shall be final
and binding on the parties. Judgment on the award may be entered in any court of competent
jurisdiction.


SECTION 11 MISCELLANEOUS

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to its subject
matter and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, proposals, and communications,
whether written or oral. No amendment or modification of this Agreement shall be effective unless in
writing and signed by both parties. No waiver of any provision of this Agreement shall be deemed a
waiver of any other provision or of any subsequent breach. If any provision of this Agreement is
held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.
This Agreement shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the parties and their respective
successors and permitted assigns. Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written
consent of the other party, except to an affiliate or in connection with a merger or sale of
substantially all assets. All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the
addresses set forth below or to such other address as a party may designate by written notice.
